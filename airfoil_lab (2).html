What you're seeing

Increasing wind speed increases aerodynamic force strongly because dynamic pressure scales with V². Camber and angle of attack change the estimated lift coefficient. Drag includes a simple profile term plus an induced-drag term.

L = ½ρV²S CL    |    D = ½ρV²S CD
